## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-prestige digital stage for modern performing arts and youth cultural mastery. Balancing centuries-old Javanese puppetry heritage with bleeding-edge product aesthetics, the identity bridges classical performance arts (*wayang kulit*, *karawitan*, *pedalangan*) and the next-generation creative economy. 

The visual style blends **Editorial Neo-Culturalism** with **Luminous Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Deep midnight indigo serves as the theatrical shadow screen (*kelir*), illuminated by radiant brass and warm parchment undertones.
- **Tone of Voice**: Reverent yet progressive, proud, cinematic, and sharply contemporary. Avoids stereotypical antique tropes in favor of sculptural silhouettes, architectural lines, and dynamic performance telemetry.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Evokes the sacred anticipation of an overture, executive polish for booking agents, and youthful prestige for emerging master puppeteers (*dalang muda*).

## Colors

The palette operates in a default cinematic dark mode, mirroring the ritual night performances of *Wayang*:

- **Primary (`#E5B842`, Supporting `#D4AF37`, `#8C6D1F`)**: *Gunungan Gold*. Represents divine illumination, brass gamelan resonance, and elite mastery. Used for prominent calls to action, golden radiant borders, accolades, and key focal interactions.
- **Secondary (`#C84B31`)**: *Kala Terracotta / Royal Crimson*. Denotes theatrical tension, fiery creative passion, vital notifications, and active performance triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: *Emerald Jade*. Reserved for artist authentication, verified master badges, booking success confirmations, and status metrics.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0F19` Base, `#131A2A` Raised, `#1E273D` Interactive)**: *Midnight Kelir Indigo*. Deep blue-black tones replace flat grayscale, infusing every surface with depth.
- **Parchment Accents (`#FDFBF7` Text Primary, `#F4EFEA` Muted Surfaces, `#94A3B8` Secondary Text)**: Soft bone-sand whites inspired by handcrafted buffalo parchment, delivering readable, high-contrast text devoid of harsh sterile white glare.

## Typography

The type system balances humanistic authority with sharp digital execution:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys ceremonial poise and dramatic storytelling. Display and headline levels capture the elegance of classical manuscript engraving, styled with high contrast and tight leading for a theatrical, editorial finish.
- **Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Anchored locally in Indonesian typographic design, this sans-serif delivers exceptional legibility on small viewports, high optical clarity on dark surfaces, and geometric crispness across UI controls and dense data tables.
- **Styling Rules**:
  - Labels and overlines leverage uppercase styling with positive letter spacing (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to mimic royal inscriptions.
  - Body text utilizes `#FDFBF7` with lowered opacity (85% for primary, 65% for secondary) to prevent eye strain against midnight backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid governs the composition:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Container max-width spans 1280px to maintain focused visual paths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom navigation zones adhere to 48px minimum hit target clearance with thumb-friendly reachability.

Vertical cadence follows an **8px structural rhythm** (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Generous spacing frames talent showcases like gallery exhibitions rather than cluttered registries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy reflects the layered dimensions of the Wayang shadow box (*kelir*, *blencong* oil lamp, puppet layers, and background):

1. **Floor (Base Canvas)**: Solid `#0B0F19` with a subtle organic grain texture (1.5% noise) evoking parchment paper.
2. **Surface Low (Containers & Tables)**: Tinted glass layer: `background: rgba(19, 26, 42, 0.70)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, edged with a refined ghost outline `1px solid rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.12)`.
3. **Surface Raised (Interactive Cards & Modals)**: `background: rgba(26, 35, 56, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, framed with `1px solid rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.25)` and an ambient, warm gold drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.08)`.
4. **Surface Pinnacle (Hero Badges & Popovers)**: Enhanced luminous bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px 2px rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.22), 0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric harmony through moderate, calculated curvatures:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to input fields, buttons, and micro tags.
- **Card Radius (`1rem` / 16px)**: Applied to profile cards, performance showcases, and floating navigation panels.
- **Container Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px)**: Applied to modals, bottom sheets, and elevated stage wrappers.
- **Geometric Motifs**: Outer structural cards may integrate subtle 45-degree chamfered top corners or hairline laser-engraved gold borders influenced by *tumpal* and *gunungan* patterns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Golden Sunburst)**: High-sheen brass gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E5B842 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #8C6D1F 100%)`), text `#0B0F19` (font weight 600). Active state triggers subtle gold rim glow.
- **Secondary (Midnight Ghost)**: Transparent fill, `1px solid rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.4)`, text `#FDFBF7`. On hover, background transitions to `rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.1)`.
- **Destructive / Urgency**: Crimson base `#C84B31` with warm glow effect for booking expirations or cancel actions.

### Chips & Cultural Tags
- Pill-shaped or subtle rounded tags (`rounded-lg`) utilizing translucent dark indigo fills (`rgba(19, 26, 42, 0.9)`) with gold micro-borders (`1px solid rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.2)`).
- Specific classification tags (e.g., *Dalang Ruwat*, *Sabetan*, *Gaya Surakarta*, *Gaya Yogyakarta*) carry unique metallic foil accent dots.

### Profile & Performance Showcase Cards
- Translucent backdrop container with 16px corner radius.
- Includes a subtle top-border highlight line (`1px solid linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #E5B842, transparent)`).
- Media headers utilize a cinematic 16:9 or 4:5 aspect ratio with bottom-to-top midnight gradient fade, ensuring text placed over artist portraits remains accessible.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.8)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(244, 239, 234, 0.15)`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#E5B842` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.15)`).
- Placeholder: `#94A3B8` (WCAG AAA compliant on midnight indigo).

### Badges & Verification Indicators
- **Verified Dalang Muda Badge**: Deep emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`), emerald border (`#10B981`), paired with an engraved brass leaf icon and golden typography.
- **Seniority / Level Badges**: Intaglio gold medal styling featuring micro serif numerals.

### Lists & Repertory Schedules
- Borderless rows separated by soft glowing hairpins (`rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.08)`).
- Hover state raises the row with a luminous indigo-to-gold gradient sweep (`rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.04)`).
